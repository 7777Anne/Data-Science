# Research review

**Status: planned, not a completed literature survey.**

Starting official source: NHTSA public recalls and complaints APIs and data dictionaries: https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis

## Questions to answer
- What precise stakeholder problem and decision are modeled?
- Which features can be observed at inference time?
- What baselines are published and fairly comparable?
- What is a valid temporal/test split?
- How will failure cases, uncertainty and costs be measured?

## Planned approach
Grounded retrieval on vehicle safety records, source citations, Recall@K, MRR, refusals and API.

## Risks
Consumer complaints are allegations, not verified defects; no proprietary Mercedes documents.
