# Conveyor maintenance (fictional demo)

If a conveyor bearing temperature exceeds 75 C, the operator must stop the conveyor, isolate the power source, and notify the maintenance supervisor. Do not restart until a qualified technician inspects the bearing and documents the corrective action.

After maintenance, record the work order ID, parts replaced, inspection result, technician name and restart approval in the maintenance log. The supervisor authorizes restart only after guards are replaced and the equipment passes a controlled test.

These instructions are fictional training data and must never replace an actual approved safety procedure.
