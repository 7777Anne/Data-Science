# Line changeover checklist (fictional demo)

Before line changeover, the operator confirms the new work order, stops the previous batch, completes line clearance and checks that the updated tooling and approved bill of materials are available. Record the current batch completion time.

During startup, perform the first-article inspection and capture the result in the shift log. The line lead approves routine production after a conforming first article is documented.

This sample document is fictional and not a real manufacturing procedure.
